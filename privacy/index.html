Privacy and corrections

1. What this site is

PoliticianWatch SA is a draft register of South Africa's elected representatives. It starts with the councillors of the City of Johannesburg elected on 4 November 2026. It aims to be neutral and sourced. It is not linked to any political party, the Electoral Commission or any council.

2. What it shows

It shows information about a representative's public role only: the name and seat as published in the Government Gazette, party, council, public offices held, career and education as publicly reported, and court matters or reported allegations that concern their public life.

It uses official public records (such as the Government Gazette, the Electoral Commission, the City of Johannesburg and court judgments) and established news media. Every item shows one of these evidence labels. Every item except an Unknown or Withheld (POPIA) one also links to its source with a date. An Unknown item has no source yet. A Withheld (POPIA) item is held back because its only source carries personal details, and no source is linked:

  • Confirmed: shown in an official or primary record.
  • Reported: reported by established news media or stated by a party.
  • Alleged: an allegation or charge that no court has decided. An allegation is not a finding.
  • Disputed: sources conflict, or a finding was challenged or set aside.
  • Unknown: not yet found in an allowed source. Nothing is guessed.
  • Withheld (POPIA): a source exists but carries personal details, so it is not published here. There is no link and the fact value is not shown.

3. What it never shows

The site never publishes home addresses, identity numbers, personal phone numbers or personal e-mail addresses, family members who are not public figures, or health information. It does not profile private individuals.

4. Allegations and right of reply

Court matters and allegations come only from court records or established news reporting, and carry their label. A news report or a court record is linked to a representative only when at least two details match. Each representative concerned will be offered a right of reply. Until a reply is recorded, the item shows "Right of reply: pending".

5. Purpose and basis

The purpose is public accountability: to help people see who represents them and what the public record says about their public role.

The lawful basis is the processing of information about public office-bearers' public role that is already in public records or published by the media, for this accountability purpose [legal wording unverified].

6. What the site does not do

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It has no forms, no accounts and no scripts. While it is a draft, it asks search engines not to index it.

7. Corrections, replies and removal

To ask for a correction or a removal, or to send a reply to an item, e-mail privacy@grokforum.site.

Please give the page link and the item you mean. Do not send identity numbers, phone numbers or home addresses.

8. How long information stays

Each build replaces the pages. Earlier versions stay in the site's public version history.

9. Who is responsible

The responsible party is GrokForum. Contact: privacy@grokforum.site.

10. Your rights

You can ask whether we hold information about you, and ask for a copy of it, by e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

You can ask for a correction of your information, or object to its processing, by e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).
